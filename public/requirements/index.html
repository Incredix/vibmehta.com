Rental requirements.

These are the same criteria for every applicant. Send this page when someone asks what you need to qualify.

Apply for this home

Income

Combined verifiable gross income of at least 3× the monthly rent. Recent pay stubs, an offer letter, or tax returns if you are self-employed.

Credit & screening

We run a credit and tenant-screening check with your written consent. We look at the full file — income, rental history, and credit — not a single cutoff score.

Who applies

Every adult 18+ who will live in the home must submit their own application. Occupants and pets belong on the form, not in a later surprise.

ID & history

Government photo ID, current address, and landlord or housing references. Honest answers on eviction, bankruptcy, and conviction questions; a yes is not an automatic no.

Lease

12 months is preferred. Six-month, month-to-month, and longer terms are considered.

Household

Smoke-free inside. Pets must be disclosed; approval is case by case. This is a 3-bed, 2-bath single-family home.

Move-in

First month’s rent plus a security deposit, as allowed by California law. There is no application fee.

Fair housing

Equal Housing Opportunity. Lawful source of income, including housing vouchers, is accepted.
